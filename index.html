How the score works. 0-10 per day for a small boat: full marks under 10 kt, minus 0.6 per knot above, minus for gusts over 25 kt and waves over 0.6 m. Wind guide: <10 kt flat · 10-15 nice · 15-20 choppy · 20+ hold on.
Bite times use the solunar method: major periods when the moon is overhead or underfoot, minor at moonrise and moonset, computed from moon age (about ±30 min). Red Sea tides at Hurghada are small (under 0.6 m), so the moon and the dawn/dusk light matter more than tide here.
